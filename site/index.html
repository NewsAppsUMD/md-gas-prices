Maryland Fuel Prices

This page shows the prices that the State of Maryland pays for fuel under its statewide fuel contract. All prices are in dollars per gallon. They are not retail pump prices.

The Maryland Department of General Services (DGS) publishes a new price table each day, Monday to Saturday. Each table gives prices for 14 combinations of fuel type and delivery method, in six regions of the state. The prices follow the OPIS daily wholesale contract price for the delivery day.

Use the controls below to select a fuel, regions and a time range. The chart and table show how prices change from day to day and from week to week.

Download the data

CSV files for every fuel, delivery type and region. Each file has a row for each fuel and region in each period.

  • Daily prices (about 3 MB). This file has one row for each published day.
  • Weekly averages (about 600 KB). The weeks run Monday to Sunday.
  • Monthly averages (about 120 KB).

Each average uses only the days that DGS published a price. The days column gives the number of days in each average, so a short first or last period is easy to see. A blank price means that the fuel is not offered in that region.

About the data

Tankwagon
Fuel delivered by a smaller tank truck.
Transport
Fuel delivered as a full tanker-truck load. The price is usually lower than tankwagon.
Consigned
Fuel that the supplier keeps in State tanks. The State pays when it uses the fuel.

DGS does not publish prices on Sunday. The Saturday price applies through Monday, so a change of zero on a Monday is normal. "—" means that the fuel is not offered in that region. A day change compares with the previous published day. Week and month changes compare with the latest published day at least 7 or 30 days earlier.

Source: Maryland Department of General Services, Daily Fuel Prices, part of the Statewide Automated Fuel Dispensing and Management System. The DGS site keeps only the last 90 days of price tables. This site keeps every table that has been collected. For older data, contact the DGS Fuel Management Department at 410-767-4310.
